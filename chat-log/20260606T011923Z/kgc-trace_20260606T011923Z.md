## 2. Options skew divergence between BTC and gold

  - **Elevated implied vol**, large and growing open interest [2][3][5].
  - Frequent **put‑skew spikes** during drawdowns — large notional spent on downside hedging [2][5][8].
  - Periods of **call‑skew** when upside lottery traits dominate [3].

- Gold options:
  - Lower vol; skew more stable.

  - Upside skew steepens mainly in **inflation or FX‑regime panic**.

Key observed divergence:

- BTC sometimes shows **rich downside skew and/or upside skew** *even as* ETFs show strong inflows (bullish narrative) [2][3][5].
- Gold may simultaneously have **relatively neutral skew** despite heavy ETF inflows (central bank + reserve flows are implemented in spot/futures, not necessarily in OTC options).

In other words, **BTC vol surface is closer to equity/tech**, while **gold vol is closer to FX/rates**.

3. **ETF‑linked options feedback loops:**

   Desks still discount **cross‑gamma effects** between BTC and gold:  
   - When macro desks are short BTC calls and short gold puts (typical “risk‑on” warehouse), a shock that hits both vol surfaces forces **synchronized hedging** (buy BTC spot/futures, buy gold), compressing relative skew faster than historical patterns suggest.
